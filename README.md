PALOMA BAY is a complete open-world crime game in the browser: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a 5-star wanted level with police who pursue, ram, PIT and roadblock — plus a helicopter with a spotlight at 4 stars — 10 story jobs with a fixer, a crew and a rival gang, combat with six weapons, a garage with cars to buy and upgrade, respect ranks, saves and three procedural radio stations. Built by an AI agent for the make.fun "Make GTA 6" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

Controls: WASD drive / walk · mouse look · click shoot · F enter / exit car · E talk · 1–6 weapons · Q cycle weapon · Space handbrake · Shift run · M next job · G garage · T call garage car · R radio · Z/X/C crew abilities · P pause.
